# Brief 4 — Promotions that fight each other

**Customer:** Ridgeline Hotels — nine venues, mostly pubs, two bistros.
**Contact:** Tania Broughton, Group Marketing & Operations Manager.
**Status:** Existing customer. Sales have had one call. There is no written requirement and
no product manager. What follows is what we have.

---

## Call notes — Monday 11:00am

*Present: [Sales], Tania Broughton. Notes are close to verbatim.*

> "We run too many promotions and the tills can't cope. That's the short version.
>
> So: happy hour is four till six, fifteen percent off all drinks, every weekday. Members
> get ten percent off everything, all the time — that's about twelve hundred people across
> the group. Schnitzel Tuesday is eighteen dollars including a pot. Staff get thirty
> percent. And Thursdays we do a 'parma and a pint for two' for fifty-five dollars, which
> the bistros hate me for.
>
> Obviously they only ever get the best single deal. We're not giving away two discounts on
> the same drink, that's how you end up selling beer at a loss.
>
> The trouble is nobody can work out what the till is going to do. The staff don't trust it,
> so they'll punch in a manual price to make it come out at what they think it should be.
> Which — look, it gets the customer served, but it means the promotion reporting is
> fiction.
>
> And there's Ray. Ray's a member, he's in every Tuesday for the schnitzel, and he expects
> his ten percent off on top of the eighteen dollars because he always has. Sixteen twenty.
> If he doesn't get it he's at the bar about it and the duty manager just overrides it. He's
> not the only one, he's just the loudest.
>
> Every time I want to change something I have to raise a ticket with you lot and wait three
> days. I had a Melbourne Cup thing last year that I gave up on because it wasn't going to
> be configured in time.
>
> I need this sorted before summer. We've got a whole program of stuff planned and at the
> moment I'm frightened of it."
